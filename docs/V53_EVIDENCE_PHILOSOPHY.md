# BENFRANK v5.3 — Two-Sided Evidence Philosophy

Additive research-only challenger to v5.2 and v5.3. Preserve frozen predictions, simulation receipts, independent Blind model, and NO_TICKET gates.

## Mandatory philosophy

1. Exact-line supremacy: analyze the uploaded threshold, not general player quality.
2. Opportunity before conversion: model minutes, usage, chances, efficiency, and variance separately.
3. Opposite-side prosecution: establish the strongest evidence-backed OVER and UNDER cases.
4. Conditional history: compare role, minutes, teammate availability, position, opponent, and pace.
5. Time integrity: exclude every source observation not available at the frozen cutoff.
6. Uncertainty honesty: UNKNOWN is not support; 25K simulations are not calibration.
7. Correlation control: 100 overlapping tests do not constitute 100 independent votes.
8. Winner preservation: retain immutable champion forecasts; challenger changes require forward validation.

## Sixty required questions — ten families, six questions each

### Exact-line distribution
- How many historical games covered, missed, or pushed the exact uploaded line?
- What do L5/L10/L15/L20/L25/L32/L52, season, previous season and available career show?
- Is the mean above the line while the median is below?
- Are outcomes concentrated near the line or in distant tails?
- Does one extreme game dominate the apparent trend?
- What happens when the closest two historical games are removed?

### Minutes and rotation
- What minutes are needed to cover at ordinary per-minute production?
- How frequently has the player reached that threshold?
- What happens with questionable teammates IN and OUT?
- How much does foul trouble reduce opportunity?
- What happens in a blowout or shortened rotation?
- What is the realistic low-minute route to the opposite side?

### Opportunity
- How many attempts, rebound chances, potential assists or possessions are required?
- How often has the player received that opportunity volume?
- How does the opponent affect those chances?
- How does teammate availability redistribute opportunities?
- Can the player cover with normal conversion?
- Which missing opportunity inputs require explicit uncertainty?

### Conversion
- What efficiency is required to cover?
- How often has the player achieved that efficiency?
- Does opponent defense affect conversion, not just opportunity?
- Were recent results driven by unsustainable teammate shooting?
- Can the player miss despite ample opportunity?
- Can the player cover with below-average efficiency?

### Matchup and position
- What does the opponent allow to the actual player role and position?
- Were past allowances driven by pace, absences, or unusual minutes?
- How did comparable players perform against this opponent?
- Are positional averages distorted by outliers?
- What changes with the upcoming projected lineup?
- Which defensive matchup inputs are verified versus unknown?

### Historical similarity
- Which earlier games are closest on pregame-observable conditions?
- How many features are shared with each comparable game?
- What were the exact-line outcomes of those games?
- Do conclusions survive removal of the closest analogues?
- Are same-player and peer-player comparisons separated?
- Does similarity add information beyond minutes and usage?

### Availability
- Which injury statuses are official, secondary, projected or unknown?
- How do questionable-player IN/OUT scenarios change the result?
- Are reserve priors observed or explicitly peer-estimated?
- Do rotations conserve 240 regulation minutes per team?
- Which availability branch reverses the selected side?
- How is model void risk distinguished from an UNDER?

### Game script
- What are the close-game and blowout pathways?
- How sensitive is the prop to foul trouble or early leads?
- Can defensive adjustments change the player's role?
- Can OVER survive a low-possession game?
- Can UNDER survive a high-possession game?
- What if the expected game environment is wrong?

### Blind and adversarial
- What does the isolated line-blind model expect?
- Which inputs explain primary-versus-Blind disagreement?
- What is the strongest evidence-backed OVER case?
- What is the strongest evidence-backed UNDER case?
- Which single assumption reverses the primary direction?
- Which stress scenarios reverse the prediction?

### Validation and release
- Were target-game outcomes excluded from all features?
- Can every exact-line count be traced to source game IDs?
- Is calibration valid for this platform, market and line band?
- Is the projected edge larger than its uncertainty?
- What observed postgame result would falsify the thesis?
- Is this RESEARCH_ONLY or genuinely ticket-eligible?

## Evidence contract

For each question record: ID, player, market, exact line, as-of cutoff, source, game IDs, sample size, observed answer, OVER case, UNDER case, missingness, correlated evidence group, scenario reversal and confidence limitation. No source means UNKNOWN, not a fabricated explanation.
